# Indian lithium-ion battery packs: export plan

Interactive export business model for HS 8507.60 (lithium-ion accumulators), built for the Trade Analytics course at Great Lakes Institute of Management, Chennai.

Live page: https://pransh18.github.io/liion-export-plan/

The page covers six steps: product and HS code, India's edge (RCA, unit value, export similarity), market choice (weighted scorecard with alternative weights), volume, money and risk (break-even and stress tests), and market access (tariffs, non-tariff measures, trade remedies, logistics).

Market data: ITC Trade Map and ITC Market Access Map, 2025-2026. Cost figures are stated assumptions.
